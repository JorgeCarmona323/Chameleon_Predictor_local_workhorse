## What's in the box

| file | job | what it answers |
|---|---|---|
| `orca_diazirine_opt_gas.inp` | `Opt Freq`, ωB97X-D3/def2-TZVP, gas | the true DFT N=N length; is it a real minimum (no imaginary freq)? |
| `orca_diazirine_opt_water.inp` | `Opt`, CPCM(water) | same N=N in implicit water (matches my water ensembles) |
| `orca_diazirine_opt_chcl3.inp` | `Opt`, CPCM(chloroform) | same N=N in implicit chloroform (matches my membrane ensembles) |
| `orca_diazirine_scan.inp` | relaxed scan, N=N 1.15→1.50 Å, 15 pts | is 1.43 Å a basin, or is there a single minimum near 1.23 Å? |

All three opt jobs should give essentially the same N=N (~1.23 Å) — that's the point: it confirms
the bond length is solvent-independent, ruling out an implicit-solvent artifact.

**Model:** 3-(trifluoromethyl)-3-phenyl-3H-diazirine (TPD) — the standard photoaffinity diazirine.
The real tag also has an alkyne handle on the aryl; it's truncated to phenyl here because it's far
from the N=N and the ring geometry is local. N atoms are 0-based indices **11 and 12** in the
geometry block (the scan already references them).

**To run:**

orca orca_diazirine_opt_gas.inp   > orca_diazirine_opt_gas.out
orca orca_diazirine_opt_water.inp > orca_diazirine_opt_water.out
orca orca_diazirine_opt_chcl3.inp > orca_diazirine_opt_chcl3.out

## How we read the result

- **Opt N=N ≈ 1.23 Å, no imaginary freq, scan shows one minimum near 1.23 Å** → GFN2's 1.43 Å is a
  **spurious artifact**. Action: the 5 FAIL ensembles are unphysical and must be re-run with an N=N
  constraint (~1.23 Å); the 3 PASS ensembles stand.
- **Opt/scan shows a real second minimum near 1.43 Å** → deeper issue; revisit the structures and
  the GFN2 protocol before trusting any diazirine ensemble.

## If we want to be airtight (heavier, optional)

The fragment settles the *geometry* question. To also check that GFN2's *energies* (Boltzmann
weights) are trustworthy on the real system, the next step would be DFT single-points (or a small
opt) on the actual macrocycle conformers — ~150 atoms, much costlier. Not needed for the
geometry verdict; only if the conformer *populations* later look suspect.
