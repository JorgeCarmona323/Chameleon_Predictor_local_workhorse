## CENSO Decision — Confirmed Not Needed for Main Pipeline

Ketzel et al. 2025 (Heterophyllin B, Yang Hu co-author) used CREST + CENSO + NMR validation on a cyclic octapeptide. Key finding: CREST GFN2-xTB correctly identified the two NMR-visible conformers. CENSO corrected energy ordering but did not change which conformers dominated.

**Conclusion for our pipeline:** GFN2-xTB Boltzmann weights are qualitatively correct for permeability-scale predictions. CENSO is needed for NMR structure determination, not population-level PSA averaging. Keep GFN2-xTB Boltzmann weighting; cite Ketzel 2025 as independent NMR validation.

CENSO is reserved for the macrocycle target-family screening workflow (post-MD, top 1–2 candidates only).
